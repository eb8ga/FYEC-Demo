# Heat-set insert press

## What it is

| Part | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Press | Vector 3D VLMP Pro heat-set insert press. A carriage on linear rails lowers a soldering iron straight down onto the insert. |
| Heat source | Handskit 927 soldering iron. Adjustable 190 to 480 °C, 100 to 200 W. Heats up in about 20 seconds. |
| Tip | CNC Kitchen insert tip. It fits inside the insert and keeps it straight. |

## What we use it for

Brass threaded inserts melt into the 3D-printed enclosure so its screws thread into metal instead of plastic. Plastic threads strip after a few uses. Inserts do not.

| Insert | Screw | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CNC Kitchen M2.5 x 4 mm](https://cnckitchenus.store/products/gewindeeinsatz-threaded-insert-m2-5-standard-100-stk-pcs) | Nylon M2.5 | Mounting the circuit boards |
| [CNC Kitchen M3 x 5 x 4 (Voron)](https://cnckitchenus.store/products/made-for-voron-gewindeeinsatz-threaded-insert-m3x5x4-100-stk-pcs) | Stainless steel M3 socket head cap screw (SHCS) | Closing the enclosure |

Enclosure material: PLA, so set the iron to about 225 °C. Hole sizes for each insert are in CNC Kitchen's design guide (linked at the bottom).

## Hazards

| Hazard | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Burns | The tip, the brass insert and the plastic around it. The tip can reach 480 °C. The insert stays hot after the press goes up. |
| Fumes | Plastic that gets too hot smokes. |
| Fire | A hot tip resting on paper, cloth or a cable. |
| Pinch point | Between the carriage and the base, and at the counterweight. |
| Electrical | Mains-powered iron. Keep the cord away from the tip. |

## Before you start

- [ ] Safety glasses on. Hair tied back, sleeves up.
- [ ] Press on a flat, stable surface with nothing flammable nearby.
- [ ] Iron cord undamaged and clear of the tip and the rails.
- [ ] Insert tip matches the insert size (M2.5 or M3) and is tight.
- [ ] Room ventilated.
- [ ] You know where the iron's switch and plug are.

## Set the temperature

Set the iron 10 to 20 °C above the plastic's printing temperature.

| Plastic | Iron temperature |
| --- | --- |
| PLA | about 225 °C |
| PETG | about 245 °C |
| ABS | about 265 °C |

Values from CNC Kitchen. If the insert sinks too fast or the plastic smokes, it is too hot. If it will not go in, it is too cold.

## How to use it

1. Set the part flat on the base, hole facing up, directly under the tip.
2. Set the insert on the hole, small end down.
3. Lower the carriage until the tip sits in the insert. Let the heat do the work. Do not force it.
4. Stop at about 90 % of the depth. Raise the carriage.
5. Press the insert the last bit flush with a flat tool, not your finger.
6. Leave the part to cool for at least 30 seconds before you touch the insert.

Keep your fingers out from under the carriage at all times.

Switching between M2.5 and M3 means changing the tip. Turn the iron off and let it cool first. A tip that looks cool can still burn.

## When you are done

1. Turn the iron off and unplug it.
2. Leave the carriage raised so the tip hangs in the air while it cools.
3. Do not touch the tip until it is cool. That takes several minutes.

## If something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You touch the tip or a hot insert | Cool the burn under running water for 20 minutes. Tell the instructor. |
| Smoke or a burning smell | Raise the carriage, turn the iron off, unplug it, ventilate. |
| Insert goes in crooked | Let it cool. Do not pull a hot insert out. Ask the instructor. |
| Cord touches the tip | Turn off and unplug. Do not use the iron until the cord is checked. |
