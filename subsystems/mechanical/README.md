## Note

Use the 3D models in ../../cad/ to check the vent, window, light pipe holes and USB-C opening against real part positions. Threaded inserts: CNC Kitchen M2.5 x 4 mm for the circuit boards, with nylon M2.5 screws, and M3 x 5 x 4 (Voron) for the cover, with stainless steel M3 socket head cap screws (SHCS). They go in with the heat-set insert press: read ../../safety/heat-set-insert-press/README.md first.
